Water & supplements

At 73 kg, a reasonable starting total-fluid target is around 2.2–2.6 L/day from drinks, plus more for sweat/heat. Creatine monohydrate: commonly 3–5 g daily; no loading needed. Drink normally and stay hydrated—no special water amount is required just because you take creatine. Protein powder is optional; use it to fill a gap in your food protein target. Consult a clinician first if you have kidney disease, are pregnant, or take relevant medications.

These checkboxes track habits; they do not prescribe supplements.
